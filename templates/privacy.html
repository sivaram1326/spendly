Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate the service: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database associated with your account and is only accessible to you when signed in. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal expense data with third parties. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting) solely to operate the service, and any such providers are bound to protect your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
